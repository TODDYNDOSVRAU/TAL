Decisions:
- Company name stays "Totally Accurate Labs", correct spelling (was "Tottally"). The user knowingly kept it despite the association with Landfall's "Totally Accurate Battle Simulator" and the risk of a trademark conflict.
- The capture app T.A.K.E.S. was renamed T.A.P.E.S.: Totally Accurate Picture Engine Studio.
- Palette: #080808, #F2F2F0, Technical Gray #6B6B6B on light and #8C8C8C on dark. Accent is Ice #7EE3EA on dark and Deep Teal #0D6C74 on light (the T.A.R.K.A.S. blue).
- On 2026-10-30 the user made the T.A.R.K.A.S. blue the T.A.L. brand accent too, replacing Signal Lime #C7FF32, which is no longer used anywhere (PDF updated). Earlier that day I had wrongly switched the T.A.R.K.A.S. accent to lime and the user corrected it. Rule: products keep their own accent; migrating a product means the T.A.L. base (neutrals, fonts, seal), not the accent. The logo never takes the accent.
- Fonts: Space Grotesk, Inter, JetBrains Mono.
- Official logo, chosen by the user on 2026-10-30 after many rounds: AB-topo-negativo.svg and AB-topo-positivo.svg in C:\dev\TAL\logo\, generated by gerar.js with letters:'top' and join:'round'. Three isometric line blocks (heights 3, 2, 1) with T, A, L in line strokes lying on the top faces, no barbs, no color (the user dropped both the lime square and the blue letters). Rejected along the way: words on the faces, Gerstner layout, OPENLAB hexagon, dot matrix. Applied on 2026-10-30 to the identity PDF (cover, logo page, closing) and to the Instagram profile and highlight images in C:\dev\TAL\instagram\.

**Why:** the user wants every product built under this identity.
**How to apply:** use this palette, these fonts and this voice in any new UI or doc in C:\dev, and don't reopen the name decision.
